Blue Caves, Paxos — watercolour, on the spot where it was painted

Painted slowly, on purpose.

A canvas starts carefully — the light worked out in advance, the perspective measured, the patient part that holds the picture up.

The rest is instinct. A horizon shifts. A colour turns up that the sketch never asked for. What comes out is quiet work with something a little wild underneath — and that's where a painting gets its character.

The subjects wander: harbours and lighthouses, gardens and old houses, a glass of wine on a warm afternoon, a wall covered in other people's graffiti. Commissions are open, so yours can wander in too.
