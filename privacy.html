Privacy.

Short version: this site has no cookies, no tracking and no ads. The only personal details it handles are the ones you type into the contact form, and they are used for one thing, which is replying to you.

Who is responsible

Nikip.Art is the portfolio of Nikip, an independent painter based in Greece. She is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach her through the contact form or on Instagram.

What the contact form collects, and why

When you send a request, the form sends your name, your email address, your message and the language of the page you used. These are used only to answer your request and, if you go ahead, to arrange your painting. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your details are never sold, shared for marketing or added to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

Form messages are delivered by Formspree, a form service based in the USA, which passes them on by email and keeps a copy in its dashboard. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security. The fonts are stored on this site, so loading a page does not contact Google or any other third party.

How long it is kept

Messages are kept for as long as it takes to answer them and complete any painting that follows, and after that only as long as tax or accounting rules require. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data held about you, correct it, have it deleted, limit how it is used or object to its use. Just send a message through the contact form. If you think your data has been mishandled, you can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Links to other sites

Links to Instagram and TikTok take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing from them is loaded on this site.
